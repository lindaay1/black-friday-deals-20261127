How this board was made

An agent found each deal and checked its price on 27 November 2026, reading the price in a deals source that names the store — a store's own Black Friday release, or a deals roundup such as BeeBulletin, TechRadar, Yahoo Shopping, Product Bible, 9to5Google, DealNews or BuzzFeed. The check time and the source are printed on every deal.

Is it really a deal? NEW LOW means the lowest price seen for that item. STRONG DEAL is a deep cut well under list. GOOD DEAL is a solid saving, not a record. ROUTINE SALE is a small cut — worth a second look before you buy. The verdict never comes from the size of the discount alone: it weighs what the source said about the price.

Prices move fast on Black Friday and a deal can sell out. The store's page is the final word — always check it before you buy. Deal Hound is not affiliated with any store named here.
